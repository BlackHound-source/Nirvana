Method

Each test engine's sensor stream is normalised with training-set bounds, passed through a deterministic complex-valued reservoir (split-step field dynamics on a 32-point grid), and mapped to RUL with ridge regression. Hyperparameters are chosen on a validation split of the training engines.

Both models are scored on the last observed cycle of each test engine against the NASA RUL targets, capped at 125. Triage bands: critical ≤ 10, high ≤ 30, medium ≤ 60 cycles. Work orders are keyed by SHA-256 of subset, engine, cycle and action, so re-running a pipeline never duplicates a dispatch.
